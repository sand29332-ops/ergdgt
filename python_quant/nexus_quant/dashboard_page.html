RL fairness: headline retired

The historical VWAP-beating claim was not reproduced broadly. The committed fair study found no consistent significant PPO edge on highvol, highvol_null or trending; PPO was worse on calm and lowvol hold-outs. A significant edge appeared only under liquidity shocks.

Source: docs/results/rl_fairness.md, 2026-09-13. Five training seeds, hold-out regimes, symmetric information, fees and queue model enabled, paired confidence intervals. The execution demo above does not reproduce that study.
